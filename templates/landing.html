Read the report

What is measured, observed, guessed and unknown, with next actions.

For advertisers with results: two questions, answered separately

What has worked is not the same as what to learn next. AdPilot keeps them apart.

Best performer

Ranks ads on measured results for your objective, only among ads with enough data. If the gap is within noise, it reports the result as inconclusive instead of crowning a winner.

Next best test

Weighs how uncertain each ad is, where its creative looks weakest, and how promising its results are. It gives a hypothesis, the metric to watch, a test budget and a stopping rule.

Find competitor videos, honestly

With an optional YouTube Data API key, AdPilot searches public videos for your product and compares their text with your concepts or ads. You can also paste a single video link; neither is required.

A public video is not proof of a paid ad, so results are labelled Relevant Public Video or Potential Advertising Creative. Only a source you record yourself can make one a Verified Paid Ad. Competitor results are never estimated from views or likes.
