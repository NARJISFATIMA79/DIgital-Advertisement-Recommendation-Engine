Competitor research optional

AdPilot searches public YouTube videos related to your product. A public video is not proof of a paid ad, and views or likes are not advertising results. To check paid status, use the Google Ads Transparency Center.
Automatic discovery is off. No YouTube API key is configured, so AdPilot will not invent results. Everything else works. Add a competitor video by link or by hand below, or set up a key (see the README).

Searches

Searches are generated from your answers. When a key is configured, the first few run automatically.

Paid-ad verification

Separate from video discovery. Matches the videos above against Google Ads Transparency Center records supplied by a third-party provider (SearchApi). Only an exact YouTube video-ID match verifies a video as a paid ad; name or text similarity never does. Unmatched videos stay Unverified and are used as public reference content.
